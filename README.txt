Elementary Poets / 小学诗词集

Unzip this folder and open index.html in your browser. No installation is needed.
解压后，用浏览器打开 index.html 即可，无需安装。

Contains poems for grades 1–6, semester filters, search, full poem reading, memorization mode, and local reading progress.
包含一年级至六年级古诗词、上下册筛选、搜索、正文、背诵模式和本地已读记录。

111 classical poems and 3 modern supplementary titles. Text variants and grade placement should be checked against the student textbook. Modern supplementary entries contain titles and authors only.
111首古诗词正文及3首现代补充篇目（仅标题和作者）。具体篇目安排与文字异文以所用课本为准。
